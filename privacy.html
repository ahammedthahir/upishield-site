Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

UPIShield is a student project. This policy explains what information the app handles when you use it.

Information we process

  • The UPI ID (VPA) you enter and the payment amount and category you choose, so that a fraud-risk score can be calculated.
  • The time of the request, which is used as one input to the risk model.

UPI ID verification

To check whether a UPI ID exists, the UPI ID is sent to a payment gateway verification service (Razorpay). Any account name returned is shown to you for confirmation and is not stored by UPIShield.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, UPI PINs, passwords or OTPs.
  • We do not move or hold money.
  • We do not sell or share your information with advertisers or other third parties.

Storage and security

Requests are processed to produce a result. Any records kept are limited to what is needed to run and improve the project and are protected with access controls. Never enter your UPI PIN or OTP into this app.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any information linked to you by writing to the email on the contact section.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
